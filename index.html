Mihon Sync

A personal build of the Mihon manga reader that keeps your library in step across your Android devices, using your own Google Drive.

What it syncs

  • Which manga are in your library, and their categories
  • Read and unread chapters, bookmarks and the last page read
  • Reading history
  • Tracker entries (AniList, MyAnimeList and others). Tracker sign-ins are not synced.

How it works

When you sign in with Google, the app creates a folder called Mihon in your Google Drive and keeps small JSON files there: one listing your library and one per manga. Each device reads and updates those files, so changes made on one device reach the others.

The app asks only for Google's drive.file permission, which lets it see and change the files it created itself. It cannot see anything else in your Drive.

There is no server: your data goes only between your devices and your Google Drive.

Privacy policy

Mihon Sync is an unofficial build and is not affiliated with the Mihon project.
