Privacy policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

Mihon Sync is a personal build of the Mihon manga reader for Android with an optional Google Drive sync. This policy explains what the app does with your data.

Data the app syncs

If you sign in to Google Drive, the app reads and writes these items so they match across your devices:

  • the manga in your library and the categories they are in
  • chapter read status, bookmarks and the last page read
  • your reading history (when you last read a chapter and for how long)
  • your tracker entries, such as status, score and chapters read on AniList or MyAnimeList

Tracker passwords and sign-in tokens are never synced.

Where it is stored

The data is stored only on your devices and in a folder called Mihon in your own Google Drive. There is no server run by the app's author, and the data is not sent to anyone else.

Google account access

The app requests only the Google Drive drive.file permission. It lets the app create files and access the files it created. The app cannot see, read or change any other files in your Google Drive. To show which account is connected, the app reads your Google account's email address. It keeps that address on your device and does not share it.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer your data, does not show ads and does not include analytics or tracking.

Removing your data

Sign out in the app under Settings → Data and storage → Google Drive sync. That removes the sign-in from the device and revokes the app's access. To delete the synced data, delete the Mihon folder from your Google Drive. You can also remove the app's access at any time from your Google account permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue on the project's GitHub page.
